# Security-automation reviewer feedback (themes)

Themed distillation of the public PR review threads on
`nick-hinds/teleport_challenge` (L3) and `tedmist1/challenge-auth0`
(L3). Both threads are reviewed primarily by **russjones**.

## Themes

- [`auth0-least-privilege.md`](auth0-least-privilege.md) — Auth0
  management scopes, Terraform provider versioning, tool-stack
  expectations.
- [`design-doc-style.md`](design-doc-style.md) — prose density and
  operational controls in the design doc.

This is a thin corpus. Two candidate threads is enough to identify
the recurring themes above but not to draw confident conclusions
about anything else. Treat these themes as load-bearing; treat the
absence of other themes as "we have not seen the data" rather than
"reviewers do not care."
